About

About

I lead PM at Pepper Content, where I build agentic AI products that have measurably changed how our content teams work. Before Pepper I was a PM and Senior Data Scientist at CommerceIQ, where I owned products managing over $750M in annual retail ad spend.

I studied engineering at BITS Pilani. I grew up in Arrah, a small city in Bihar, in a family of small business owners. That shaped how I think about who AI products are actually for.

I write Weekly AI Digest because the gap between what is shipping in AI and what most professionals use to do their jobs keeps widening. The newsletter is my attempt to close some of it.
